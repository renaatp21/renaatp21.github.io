Personal rclone client

This is a private OAuth client used by one person to connect their own Google Drive to the open-source tool rclone on their own computer. It is not a public service.

Privacy policy

This client is used only by its owner. Google Drive data is transferred directly between Google and the owner's computer. It is not sent to any server operated for this client, and it is not collected, sold or shared with anyone.

Questions can be raised through the GitHub profile of renaatp21.
